# Presenter run — From Claim to Confidence

**Audience:** senior commercial leadership (limited insurance knowledge). **Length:** 20 min.
**Rule:** a business result inside 3 minutes; an observable proof every 2–3 minutes; no
compulsory actuarial jargon. App: `https://claim-to-confidence-7474656169654171.aws.databricksapps.com`.

Explain "reserve" once: *an insurer's estimate of money it still expects to pay for claims
that have already happened.* Then never re-explain it.

## The spine (what the audience should remember)

1. A powerful model can speed up a *task*. A governed platform connects the whole *decision*.
2. Bricksurance turns one claim correction into a traceable reserve decision — with the
   reinsurance, finance and capital consequences all visible.
3. It stays pleasant for the specialists while the system enforces quality, authority and
   reproducibility underneath.

## First-business-result guarantee
Screen A paints the hero numbers within ~1 second of a warm warehouse (the count-up is a
visual reveal, not a running job). If the warehouse is cold, the first query wakes it —
open the app a minute before presenting, or keep it warm.

## Honesty notes for the presenter (not for the public screens)
- The capital / IFRS 17 step is deliberately shown as **"dependency identified"**, not a
  number — we do not fabricate a statutory figure.
- The negative test currently uses a labelled role harness; separately-authenticated
  principals are the next phase.
- The competitive framing (versus the spreadsheet walkthrough) stays cooperative and lives
  only in these notes — never on a public screen or in an export.
